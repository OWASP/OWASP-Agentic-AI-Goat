Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
New hires must complete 90 days before applying for remote work.

Core Hours
Remote employees must be online during core hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST.

Security Requirements
VPN is mandatory for accessing internal tools.
Confidential data must not be accessed on personal devices.
